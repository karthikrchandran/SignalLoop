SignalLoop UX Design Direction

The revised MVP design direction is simple: build a web-first control tower for outreach operations. The experience should make automation visible, understandable, and safe for non-technical operators.

Primary Direction

  • Use a responsive web admin experience, not a native mobile app
  • Center the product around campaign operations, lead timelines, and handoff readiness
  • Make reason codes and next actions visible wherever automation decisions appear
  • Keep the interface calm and readable under operational pressure

What The MVP Must Show Clearly

  • What the system just did
  • Why it did it
  • What it plans to do next
  • What the operator can do if recovery is needed

Design Priorities

  • Fast campaign setup and contact intake
  • Operational dashboard with queue health and KPI visibility
  • Lead timeline with reason codes and booking status
  • Handoff packet that helps the sales engineer prepare quickly
  • Accessibility and responsive behavior across desktop and mobile browser widths

Explicitly Deferred

The earlier exploration included additional ideas that are no longer part of the MVP design direction.

  • Native mobile app flows
  • Sales-engineer feedback workflows
  • Template-insights analytics and outcome tracking
  • Advanced experimentation and optimization surfaces

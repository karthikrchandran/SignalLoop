Commercial mechanics · AI agents, add-ons and guardrails

Price the job. Meter the consumption.

Bill published production workers with distinct jobs, permissions, knowledge, workflows and monitoring. Do not bill drafts, disabled agents, tests, language variants, voices or personas separately.

Competitive hybrid model

Include productive agent capacity in every SignalLoop tier, then charge for additional deployed workers. Keep model and channel consumption metered.

Platform + production agents + provider usage + support

Market signal

AiSDR starts at $250/month; Agent Frank is $499/month; HubSpot's prospecting agent consumes 100 credits per lead. SignalLoop should lead with transparent capacity, not hidden usage.

Commercial guardrails

  • Keep separate SKUs, entitlements, data boundaries and termination rights.
  • Apply discounts only to recurring software, never setup, usage, cloud or services.
  • Use annual commitments; month-to-month is 15% higher.
  • Never promise unlimited voice, SMS, WhatsApp, email, storage, models or engineering.
  • Publish Enterprise only as “from” until operating evidence supports firm limits.
  • Review tenant gross margin monthly and adjust envelopes at renewal.
